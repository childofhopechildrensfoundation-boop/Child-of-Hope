Our Programs

Comprehensive programs addressing the root causes of childhood vulnerability and poverty.

What We Do

Our Core Programs

Each program is designed with community input and evidence-based best practices to support children and families holistically.

Education

We provide quality education, school supplies, scholarships, tutoring support, and learning materials to ensure every child has access to quality education.

  • School supplies & uniforms
  • Scholarship support
  • Tutoring programs
  • Teacher training
Learn More

Healthcare

We support children and families with preventive care, medical screenings, treatment access, and compassionate HIV-related healthcare support.

  • Medical checkups & screenings
  • Vaccination outreach
  • HIV support & monitoring
  • Health education
Learn More

Nutrition

Our nutrition work helps children stay healthy, attend school regularly, and grow physically and mentally through adequate meals and support.

  • School meals
  • Supplementary feeding
  • Nutrition education
  • Family food support
Learn More

Child Protection

We help protect children from abuse and exploitation while providing counselling, case management, and safe recovery pathways.

  • Safeguarding & monitoring
  • Counselling services
  • Family support
  • Trauma recovery
Learn More

Shelter & Support

We are building pathways to safe, stable living for children in crisis through shelter support, family strengthening, and practical care.

  • Emergency shelter
  • Food & essentials
  • Case management
  • Skills & independence
Learn More

Community Development

We strengthen families and communities through awareness, empowerment, and sustainable support that creates safer environments for children.

  • Capacity building
  • Parenting & family support
  • Economic empowerment
  • Volunteer networks
Learn More

How We Create Impact

Our programs are designed based on community needs and evidence-based practices to ensure maximum impact and sustainability.

Community-Centered

We work closely with communities to understand their needs and develop culturally appropriate solutions.

Evidence-Based

Our programs are designed using research and best practices to ensure effectiveness and measurable outcomes.

Sustainable

We focus on building local capacity and creating lasting change that extends beyond our direct support.
