Child Protection

Safeguarding vulnerable children and healing trauma.

Every Child Deserves Safety and Care

Many vulnerable children face abuse, exploitation, and trauma. Without protection and support, they struggle to recover and build healthy futures. These children need safe spaces, counseling, and advocates who believe in their potential.

At Child of Hope Children's Foundation, we provide comprehensive child protection services, counselling, family support, and safe referral pathways so children can recover from trauma and rebuild their lives.

Our Goal

Protect vulnerable children from abuse and exploitation while giving them safe spaces, emotional support, and the care needed to heal, recover, and thrive.

Our Vision

We envision a future where every child grows up in a safe, supportive environment free from abuse, exploitation, and trauma.

  • Monitor and safeguard children from abuse and exploitation
  • Provide psychosocial counseling for trauma recovery
  • Offer case management services for child welfare
  • Support family reunification when safe and appropriate

Children in our protection programs recover, build resilience, and reclaim their childhood.

Why Protection Matters

Children who receive protection and support are more likely to:

  • Recover from trauma and build emotional resilience
  • Return to school and focus on their future
  • Build healthy relationships and trust
  • Develop self-confidence and self-worth
  • Break cycles of abuse and create better futures
