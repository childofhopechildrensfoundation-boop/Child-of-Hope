Shelter & Support

Providing safe haven and pathways to sustainable living.

Every Child Deserves a Safe Home

Every child deserves a safe place to grow, learn, and dream. Unfortunately, many vulnerable children in our communities face unstable living conditions that limit their opportunities.

At Child of Hope Children's Foundation, we are building practical support for vulnerable children through emergency assistance, family strengthening, mentorship, and partnerships that promote safe, stable, and nurturing environments.

Together, we can help create a future where every child grows up with hope, dignity, and the opportunity to thrive.

Our Goal

Provide emergency shelter and comprehensive support services that help children in crisis find stability, regain hope, and build sustainable paths toward independence.

Our Vision

We envision communities where no child lives without safe shelter and where every young person has the skills and support to build a stable, independent life.

  • Provide emergency shelter for children in immediate danger
  • Deliver case management for long-term stability
  • Offer livelihood training for economic empowerment
  • Support family reunification when safe and appropriate

Children with shelter and support transition to sustainable living and bright futures.

Why Shelter Matters

Children with safe shelter and support are more likely to:

  • Find stability and peace after trauma and uncertainty
  • Return to school and pursue education
  • Develop skills and prepare for employment
  • Build healthy relationships and trust
  • Create sustainable, independent futures
