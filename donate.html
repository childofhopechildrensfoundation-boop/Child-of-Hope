Donation FAQ

Common questions about giving

Is my donation secure?

Yes! We use Pesapal, a trusted payment processor, to securely handle all transactions. Your financial information is encrypted and protected.

Will I receive a receipt?

Yes! A donation receipt will be sent to your email immediately after your payment is processed. You can use this for tax purposes.

Can I donate monthly?

Currently, we process one-time donations. For recurring gifts, please contact us at childofhopechildrensfoundation@gmail.com

Where does my money go?

100% of donations support our programs: education, healthcare, nutrition, and child protection. We're committed to transparency and impact.
